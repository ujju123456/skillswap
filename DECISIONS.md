# Product decisions

## DP1 - Rejection

When a creator declines a request, the client sees the booking marked **Declined** in My bookings. The original gig remains visible and bookable because one declined brief does not mean the creator is unavailable for every project; it also keeps discovery open instead of treating a rejection as a dead end.

## DP2 - Double booking

A gig may receive multiple pending booking requests, and the creator chooses which requests to accept. This fits creative work better than an instant checkout model: a creator needs to inspect scope, timing, and fit before committing. Accepted requests stay visible, so the prototype favors transparent, creator-led scheduling over an artificial single-slot constraint.

## DP3 - Discovery

Gigs are initially shown newest-first, with the latest published gig at the top of Browse. This gives new creators a fair chance to be seen and makes the marketplace feel active; clients can then narrow the list using search and categories rather than a hidden popularity algorithm.
